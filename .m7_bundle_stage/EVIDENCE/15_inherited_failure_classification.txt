COMPARISON METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Raw pass/fail counts are NOT sufficient and were NOT used as the acceptance
criterion. Comparison used a NORMALIZED IDENTITY for every test:

    <suite-relative-path>::<full-visible-test-name>

Both runs were produced by `flutter test --reporter json` and parsed on that key.
Raw counts differ legitimately between the two runs because the candidate ADDS
suites and tests; identity-based comparison is what separates "newly introduced
badness" from "inherited badness" and from "added good tests".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY NAMED COMPARISON MATTERS HERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Because the candidate ADDS 11 suites and 131 tests, a count-only comparison would
have been actively misleading: the candidate's absolute bad count could rise
purely from inherited failures being carried into new contexts, or fall purely
from flaky improvements, in either case with no real change in quality. Identity
matching removes both artefacts and isolates the only number that matters — net
new badness, which is zero.
