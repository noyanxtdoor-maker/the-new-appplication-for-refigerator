PHASE 7 - MAPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Opened via the Maps tab. State = NORMAL BROWSING (satellite map type, owner's
markers, saved-place boundary overlay). No boundary-drawing or pin-placement
mode active at the time of the evidence capture.

1. SDK-NATIVE COMPASS = PRESENT AND RENDERED ON THE DEVICE
   The Google Maps Android SDK compass button is visible at the top-left of the
   map surface: white circular button, red north pointer with grey south tail.
   Evidence: maps_compass_topleft_zoom4.png (4x LANCZOS crop of the framebuffer).
   This is the SDK's own control, not app chrome - see item 4.

2. MAPS LOCATE ME = PRESERVED AS ITS OWN SEPARATE FEATURE
   The floating control column at the right edge contains exactly three FABs,
   confirmed from the pinned source (google_maps_surface.dart:1600-1638):
     - key 'maps-drop-pin-button'  Icon(Icons.location_pin)      tooltip 'Drop pin'
     - key 'maps-type-button'      Icon(Icons.layers_outlined)   tooltip 'Map type'
     - key 'maps-locate-button'    Icon(Icons.my_location)       tooltip 'Locate me'
   All three are visible in maps_right_fabs_zoom3.png. Locate me was NOT used as
   the compass test; it is reported here only as a preserved separate feature.

3. NO CUSTOM FACE NORTH BUTTON
   grep over lib/features/maps for compassEnabled|faceNorth|Face North|bearing|
   rotateGestures|tiltGestures|northUp returns ZERO control tokens - the only hit
   is a doc comment at google_maps_surface.dart:148 ("without changing zoom,
   bearing, or tilt"). The app adds no compass/orientation control of its own.

4. TWO-FINGER ROTATE + COMPASS TAP = NOT PERFORMED (requires physical input)
   adb 'input' is single-pointer only: it cannot synthesise the two-finger
   rotate gesture. A single-pointer tap at the compass location (device 91,414)
   was delivered and produced no visible camera or compass change, which is
   consistent with a camera that is already north-up (tapping a compass on a
   north-up camera is a no-op). It is NOT claimed as a pass.

   An earlier stray tap did enter the app's Drop Pin placement mode
   ("Move map to place pin"); this was cancelled with the X control and normal
   browsing state was restored and re-captured. No pin was placed, no boundary
   was drawn, and nothing was saved.

   Verdict: MAPS AUTO FACE NORTH = SDK-NATIVE COMPASS PRESENT AND VISIBLE;
   the interactive rotate-and-tap step is PENDING OWNER (see final report).
