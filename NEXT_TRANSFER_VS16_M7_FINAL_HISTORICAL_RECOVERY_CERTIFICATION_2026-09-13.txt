FINDING — the readout IS rendered; it is NOT missing from the widget tree.

  * The whole indicator (label + dot + line) sits behind ONE gate
    (`indicatorVisible`). A gate cannot hide the label alone, so the owner's
    "dot + line visible, text not" CANNOT be a gate failure.
  * The readout renders at an EFFECTIVE 8.13 logical px (7.18 px for a
    4-digit-hour label), because `FittedBox.scaleDown` shrinks the declared
    `fontSize: 15` by 0.54x. The shrink is forced by the label `Container`
    declaring `height: capsuleHeight = 18` inside a band of only
    `indicatorHeight = 12`.
  * The ambient hour labels render at `fontSize: 11` with NO scaling.
    => the "highlighted" current-time readout is drawn SMALLER than the hour
    labels it sits beside. That is the opposite of the recorded CT-02/CT-03
    intent in the source comment at planner_screen.dart:4880-4889: "The time text
    itself is the highlighted element - semantic primary, larger (fontSize 15) in
    a 62dp leftward-widened area with 4dp padding."
  * It is positioned at x in [3.17, 60.83] - INSIDE the 56 px hour-gutter column
    (grid starts at x=12; gutter spans 12..68) - the same region where the hour
    labels are painted, immediately left of the dot.

CONCLUSION: the readout is present but rendered at roughly two-thirds the size of
the surrounding hour labels, inside the hour-gutter column and immediately left of
the dot, so on a physical device it does not read as a distinct current-time
readout. This is a PROPERTY OF THE ACCEPTED CT-03 IMPLEMENTATION, not an M7
regression.

NO RESTORATION PERFORMED. Changing `capsuleHeight`, the `FittedBox`, or the label
geometry would be a redesign of frozen Planner code, which the handoff forbids
unless historical evidence proves the accepted readout differs from the current
source - which it does not.

HONEST CAVEAT: the measured 8.13 px uses the flutter_test fallback font, which
renders every glyph at roughly `fontSize` width, so the WIDTH constraint binds
harder than on device. On device, proportional Roboto is narrower, the height
constraint (12/18 = 0.667) binds instead, and the readout renders at about
15 x 0.667 = 10 logical px. Either way it is smaller than the 11 px hour labels.
The on-device smoke is the confirmation step.
