FINDING — the readout IS rendered; it is NOT missing from the widget tree.

  * The whole indicator (label + dot + line) sits behind ONE gate
    (`indicatorVisible`). A gate cannot hide the label alone, so the owner's
    "dot + line visible, text not" CANNOT be a gate failure.

  * The readout renders at an EFFECTIVE 8.13 logical px (7.18 px for a
    4-digit-hour label), because `FittedBox.scaleDown` shrinks the declared
    `fontSize: 15` by 0.54x. The shrink is forced by the label `Container`
    declaring `height: capsuleHeight = 18` inside a band of only
    `indicatorHeight = 12` (and, in the test font, by the 62 px label box being
    narrower than the unscaled text).

  * The ambient hour labels render at `fontSize: 11` with NO scaling.
    => the "highlighted" current-time readout is drawn SMALLER than the hour
    labels it sits beside. That is the opposite of the recorded CT-02/CT-03
    intent in the source comment at planner_screen.dart:4880-4889 — "The time
    text itself is the highlighted element — semantic primary, larger (fontSize
    15) in a 62dp leftward-widened area with 4dp padding."

  * It is positioned at x in [3.17, 60.83], i.e. INSIDE the 56 px hour-gutter
    column (the grid starts at x=12; the gutter spans 12..68) — the same region
    where the hour labels are painted, immediately left of the dot.

CONCLUSION

The current-time readout is present but rendered at roughly two-thirds the size of
the surrounding hour labels, inside the hour-gutter column and immediately left of
the dot, so on a physical device it does not read as a distinct current-time
readout. This is a PROPERTY OF THE ACCEPTED CT-03 IMPLEMENTATION, not an M7
regression: the source is byte-identical to the accepted checkpoint.

Per the handoff, NO restoration is performed. Changing `capsuleHeight`, the
`FittedBox`, or the label geometry would be a redesign of frozen Planner code, and
the handoff forbids that unless historical evidence proves the accepted readout
differs from the current source — which it does not.

HONEST CAVEAT

The measured 8.13 px is taken with the flutter_test fallback font, which renders
every glyph at roughly `fontSize` width and therefore makes the WIDTH constraint
bind harder than it will on device. On the device, proportional Roboto is narrower,
so the height constraint (12 / 18 = 0.667) binds instead and the readout renders at
approximately 15 x 0.667 = 10 logical px. Either way the readout is smaller than
the 11 px hour labels. This probe cannot measure the device font directly; the
on-device smoke is the confirmation step.
